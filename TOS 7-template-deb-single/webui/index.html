My Application

Placeholder WebUI shipped with the TOS 7 Deb single-package template.

Replace the files in webui/, then rebuild the archive with:

tar -cjf webui.bz2 -C webui/ .

webui.bz2 is required whenever config.ini sets "type": "iframe" or "open_path": true. It is decompressed by DEBIAN/postinst into /usr/local/<appid>/.
